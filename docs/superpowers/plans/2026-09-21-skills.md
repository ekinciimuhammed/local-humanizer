## Review focus
- Upstream license and commit attribution match the actual vendored files; original examples are not executable instructions.
- Custom imports cannot override built-ins, escape instruction budgets or cause HTML/code execution.
- Old settings acquire defaults without changing connection credentials or model choices.
- Enabled instructions reach every chunk, and core facts/language constraints remain authoritative.
- Writing notes cannot count code/quotes as robotic prose or masquerade as detector scores.
