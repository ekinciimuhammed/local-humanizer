Artificial intelligence is now a part of both daily life. By taking over tasks that are repeated over and over again and by helping with choices artificial intelligence is changing the way people and companies work.

One major advantage of intelligence is better efficiency. Jobs that used to take a lot of time and hard work can now be done quicker with systems. This lets workers spend their time on creative, thoughtful and important tasks.

As more people use artificial intelligence there are serious issues to think about. Problems, like keeping data unfair decisions made by machines losing jobs because of automation and not being sure if automated choices are correct are still being discussed. Companies need to think about both the good things and the dangers when they decide to use artificial intelligence.

Artificial intelligence can bring a lot of value if it is used the way. By mixing technology with ethical ideas and making sure people are involved companies can use artificial intelligence while also cutting down on possible bad results.
