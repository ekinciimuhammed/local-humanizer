# Blind app-pair meaning review

Reviewed only `blind-app-pairs.json` for this task. Punctuation is irrelevant to the verdicts. A PASS permits minor wording drift; a FAIL identifies a material alteration of a claim, limitation, or qualification.

| Label | Important meaning | Principal finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| N | PASS | All substantive claims survive; current importance and possible harm are slightly less precise. |
| O | PASS | Evidence, counts, attribution, and inference limits survive; a management plan becomes a firmer future statement. |
| P | PASS | Main claims and responsible-use qualification survive, with some narrowed or softened wording. |
| Q | FAIL | Possible lack of representativeness becomes certain exclusion, attributed to the library without support. |
| R | PASS | Survey evidence and all important limitations survive, including the management plan. |
| S | FAIL | Potential significant value becomes an affirmative conditional outcome; task-completion capability also becomes an asserted outcome. |

## N — PASS

**Coverage:** Both professional and everyday life remain. The effect on individuals and organizations, repetitive-task automation, complex-decision support, efficiency, the historical time/manual-effort comparison, and the resulting opportunity for employees to do creative, strategic, high-value work all survive. Growing adoption, all four risk categories, ongoing debate, and organizations' careful consideration of opportunities and risks remain. Responsible implementation still qualifies substantial possible value; innovation, ethics, human oversight, and harm reduction survive.

**Minor issues:** “People now use” is weaker than “integral.” “How ... work” is slightly narrower than “operate.” “Remain problems?” can presuppose existing problems, but the surrounding sentences still frame these categories as concerns under debate, not proven realized harms. “Reducing negative effects” omits “potential,” making possible harms less explicitly hypothetical. “Can create a lot of value if” appropriately retains capability and conditionality. No material invented names, numbers, attribution, results, or guarantees.

## O — PASS

**Coverage:** Bellfield Design, a recent internal hybrid-work survey, 84 invited employees, 56 respondents, and 39 within that respondent group reporting easier concentration at home are accurate. The concentration result remains a self-report and a tentative inference about some employees, not organizational productivity evidence. Both communication findings retain respondent attribution. The absence of a work-quality comparison survives. Three more months of the current schedule, further feedback, voluntary participation, self-report and nonresponse limitations, caution, and the need for more evidence on overall performance all remain.

**Minor issues:** “Management will keep” is firmer than “Management plans to retain”; use “plans to keep” for exactness. This is ordinary shorthand for the stated plan, without a conflicting new decision or completed action. “Productivity has improved” adds a temporal before/after flavor to “improves productivity,” but still explicitly denies proof. “Some” loses the slight quantity implication of “several,” without inventing a number. “Others found” is a little less explicitly reported than “Others reported,” but the survey context preserves attribution. No critical omission or unsupported factual addition.

## P — PASS

**Coverage:** Professional and everyday scope, changing operations, repetitive-task automation, complex-decision assistance, efficiency, formerly substantial time/manual effort, faster completion, and the resulting worker opportunity all survive. Creative, strategic, and higher-value work remain. Growing use, all four concerns, continuing debate, careful benefits/risks consideration, conditional responsible value, innovation, ethics, oversight, and harm mitigation are represented.

**Minor issues:** “People now use” weakens “integral.” “Companies” narrows “organizations” in the transformation and adoption-advice claims, though the final paragraph retains organizations. “Creative, strategic, or provides more value” presents work categories as alternatives where the source lists them together; it still preserves the employee opportunity across all three kinds of work. “Chances for success” is narrower than opportunities, and “very useful” is a less exact rendering of significant value. “Lower the chance of negative effects” specifies probability reduction, whereas the source allows reducing severity or extent too. “As more people use” narrows growing use to growing user numbers. None removes the important meaning or invents an established success result.

## Q — FAIL

**Critical change:** The source says responses **might not represent residents who do not attend**. The candidate says people who do not visit the library **“won't be represented in the responses.”** This replaces an uncertain representativeness limitation with certain exclusion. Nonparticipants not submitting responses is different from their interests or experiences being unrepresented by the responses. The draft also attributes this definite conclusion to the library through **“they acknowledge,”** an acknowledgment not stated in the source. Minimal repair: “although the responses might not represent residents who do not attend.”

**Other scope/strength issues:** “People who do not visit the library” can mean nonusers generally rather than residents who do not attend the pilot. “All current daytime services will stay the same” promises unchanged services, whereas the source only promises continued availability during the six weeks. Use “Existing daytime services would remain available throughout the trial.” “Starting the trial” is not exactly “approval of the pilot,” though the no-permanent-guarantee conclusion survives. Future “will” clauses are reasonably read within the expressly conditional proposal, so these are not independently treated as assertions that approval has occurred.

**Coverage otherwise:** Northbridge Library; proposed status; six weeks; October 5; Tuesday/Thursday opening until 9 pm instead of 6 pm; $4,800 already allocated for additional staffing; access for those unable to visit during working hours; no Sundays; no decision on continuation; visitor counts during added hours; optional feedback; demand assessment; end-of-pilot committee review of attendance, staffing costs, and feedback; deciding whether to recommend permanence; and no guarantee of a lasting extension are retained. There is no invented approval or incorrect numerical detail.

## R — PASS

**Coverage:** Bellfield Design and the survey's internal hybrid-work setting are accurate. The 84/56/39 counts and respondent denominator survive exactly. Easier concentration remains attributed to respondents and supports only tentative concentration benefits for some employees. The draft denies established organizational productivity improvement. Both the informal-communication difficulty and usefulness of scheduled online meetings retain employee attribution. The absent work-quality comparison remains explicit. Management still **plans** another three months of the current schedule while gathering feedback. Voluntary participation, self-reported evidence, omitted nonrespondents, cautious interpretation, and further evidence before overall-performance conclusions all survive.

**Minor issues:** “A few” slightly changes “several,” without claiming an exact count. “Productivity has gone up” gives the unestablished productivity claim a temporal comparison absent from the source. “Meetings kept them up to date” is slightly stronger than “helped them stay informed,” but remains attributed to employees. No critical scope, certainty, attribution, or numerical errors.

## S — FAIL

**Critical loss of potential-value qualification:** “Value comes when these new tools are used with a focus on ethics and human supervision” asserts value as the outcome when those conditions hold. The source says AI **has the potential** to create **significant** value when responsibly implemented. The candidate loses the explicit possibility qualification and significant scale. Responsible use is only implicit, through ethics and supervision. Minimal repair: “Used responsibly, AI could create substantial value. By combining technological innovation, ethical considerations, and human oversight, organizations can reduce potential negative effects.”

**Additional strengthening:** Tasks **“are now done faster by smart software”** changes the source's **“can now be completed faster with intelligent systems”** from capability to an asserted realized result, and makes software the performer rather than retaining assisted completion. Use “can now be completed faster with AI.” This supports the failure verdict alongside the value claim.

**Coverage otherwise:** Time/manual-effort history, repetitive work, difficult-decision assistance, change in how people and companies work, employee time for creative/strategic/higher-value activities, work and daily-life presence, growing use, all four concerns, ongoing debate, and weighing advantages against risks survive. The question format appropriately represents uncertain concerns rather than reversing affirmative evidence. Ethics, supervision, and reducing the chance of adverse results remain.

**Other minor issues/omissions:** “A part” weakens “integral.” “Companies” narrows “organizations.” “Boring” is an unsupported subjective addition; “smart software” narrows the technology category; “formulas” is a rough substitute for algorithms. The adoption context and careful consideration are less explicit. “New tools” approximates technological innovation. “Lower the chance” narrows reduction of negative effects to probability. None of these alone is the principal failure.

## Grammar, separate from meaning

N and P have awkward placement in “finish/complete tasks faster that ...”; place the relative clause next to “tasks.” Q's “what the staffing cost” is awkward; “staffing costs” is clearer. S's “hand-work” and “formulas have unfair prejudices” are unnatural. O and R are readily readable. These wording issues and all punctuation choices were excluded from the meaning verdicts.
