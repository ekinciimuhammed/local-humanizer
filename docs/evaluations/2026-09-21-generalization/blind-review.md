# Blind meaning review

Reviewed `corpus.json` and only the `source` and `output` text of all eight rewrites. Recipe labels identify files; they did not determine judgments. No prompts, detector results, preservation assessments, or other evaluation directories were consulted. PASS means all important claims, qualifications, scope, and causal links survive. FAIL means at least one substantive change; it does not mean the whole rewrite is poor. Minor wording changes are distinguished below.

| Output | Important meaning | Concrete findings | Minor issues |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `remote-work-existing.json` | **FAIL** | “an important part” becomes “rely on,” adding operational dependence. Recruitment “beyond their immediate geographic area” becomes “from anywhere,” an unsupported expansion. “A flexible arrangement” becomes “Letting people choose their setup,” adding employee choice. Most workspace, management, communication, and support caveats survive. | “Organizations” narrows to “companies”; “clear expectations” narrows to “clear goals”; “information” narrows to “data.” |
| `remote-work-general.json` | **FAIL** | “an important part of how many organizations operate” becomes “Many organizations now rely on remote work to operate.” Reliance is stronger than importance: an organization may make substantial use of remote work without depending on it to operate. The remaining substantive benefits, geographic scope, contextual conditions, unequal impacts, and limits of mitigation are preserved. | Benefits become declarative (“gives employees”), but the following sentence explicitly retains their conditional realization. “Can be effective” is a slightly stronger description than “may be useful,” while retaining possibility and the support condition. |
| `compost-existing.json` | **FAIL** | “quality of the finished compost” becomes “how good the final soil is,” changing the product whose quality organizers should evaluate. Unsuitable compostable packaging is narrowed to packaging that “might not break down,” inventing a particular reason for unsuitability. “Deal with contamination” becomes “removing trash,” narrowing the possible contaminants and remedies. The participation, cost, volume caveat, resident instructions, volunteer reliance, and poor-handling risks largely remain. | “Regular maintenance” becomes “constant upkeep”; “should examine” becomes “must look”; “difficult to sustain” becomes possible outright failure. The opening also strengthens a possible benefit to a direct result, partially tempered by the next sentence. |
| `compost-general.json` | **FAIL** | “can reduce” landfill food waste and “provide material that helps improve soil” become “improves soil and keeps food waste out of landfills.” This converts possible benefits into asserted effects and collapses producing a useful soil amendment into actually improving soil. The next sentence retains dependence on inputs and management, which mitigates but does not fully restore the original claim. All later substantive requirements, risks, expansion criteria, and reporting guidance survive. | “Volunteers can perform” is slightly stronger than “can support” the tasks; no concrete unsupported example or number is added. |
| `software-cache-existing.json` | **FAIL** | “can reduce” database waiting and “can improve response times” become definite “cuts down” and “makes the app feel faster.” “Does not necessarily help requests that mostly access new data” becomes “doesn't really help if the app mostly looks for new data”: a qualified request-level limitation becomes a broad app-level dismissal. “How much temporary inconsistency” is tolerable becomes “if” the app tolerates “some brief mismatches,” losing the degree of tolerance needed to choose a strategy. Invalidation, stale data despite a correct database, memory cost, expiry/request tradeoff, realistic workloads, and correctness monitoring remain. | “Feel faster” substitutes subjective experience for response time; “too old” is narrower than “no longer valid.” |
| `software-cache-general.json` | **PASS** | Preserves repeated-data benefits, “does not necessarily” help new-data requests, memory use, invalidation difficulty, stale reads despite a correct database, expiry versus request-volume tradeoff, realistic workload measurement, change frequency, and the level of tolerated temporary inconsistency. No substantive facts or examples are added. | “Which improves response times” is more categorical than “This can improve,” but remains attached to the opening possibility of reducing database waiting. Monitoring “should” becomes “must,” modestly strengthening the recommendation. |
| `turkish-library-existing.json` | **FAIL** | “çevrimiçi ziyaret sayısının” (online visit count) becomes “internet sitesine giriş yapanların sayısının” (number of people entering the website), changing the metric. “ihtiyaç duydukları kaynaklara” (resources they need) narrows to “istedikleri belgelere” (documents they want). “Personelin bakım yükünü” (staff maintenance burden) broadens to how much staff struggle running the system. The original caveat applies even when the record **is** current (“güncel olması”); “güncel görünse” weakens this to appearing current. The access disparity, telephone/staff alternatives, continued face-to-face support, and joint system-selection considerations otherwise survive. | “Korunması önemlidir” (preservation is important) becomes “devam etmesi gerekir” (must continue), strengthening the recommendation. |
| `turkish-library-general.json` | **FAIL** | “zaman kazanmasına yardımcı olabilir” (may help save time) becomes “zaman kazanmasını sağlar” (ensures/enables saving time), removing the possibility qualification. The current-record caveat also changes from “güncel olması” (being current) to “güncel görünmesi” (appearing current); this no longer explicitly warns that an actually current record still does not guarantee shelf availability. The remaining access disparity, alternative support, maintenance burden, accuracy/usability criteria, and visit-count limitation are preserved. | The final sentence drops “ihtiyaç duydukları” (they need) before resources, a small loss of specificity. No new concrete examples or facts are added. |

Under this strict preservation standard, the general rewrites pass 1 of 4 examples; the existing rewrites pass 0 of 4. The general rewrites usually retain more of the source's detail, but these outputs do not establish that the general recipe is ready to replace the existing one without further correction and evaluation. The recurring concern is strengthened certainty; remote-work dependence and the Turkish current-record caveat also need attention. This conclusion concerns only these eight outputs, not expected behavior on unseen text.
